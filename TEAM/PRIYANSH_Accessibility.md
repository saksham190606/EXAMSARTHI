# Priyansh — Accessibility & Research

## Main Responsibility

Make sure EXAMSARTHI is genuinely usable for visually impaired users.

## Your Tasks

* Research basic WCAG/accessibility requirements.
* Check keyboard navigation.
* Check screen-reader compatibility.
* Check text size, contrast and focus indicators.
* Try the website without using a mouse.
* Find accessibility problems.
* Tell Saksham what needs to be fixed.

## What You Give the Team

* Accessibility checklist
* List of problems found
* Suggestions for improvement

## Important

You don't need to code. Your job is to test and improve the accessibility of the product.
